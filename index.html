FAQ

Quick Questions

Do you deliver and pick up?

Yes! We deliver and pick up your rented equipment around Santa Maria, Bulacan and nearby areas. Delivery fees may depend on your location, so send us your address for an exact quote.

How early should I book?

We recommend booking at least a week ahead. For weekends, holidays, and peak seasons like December and fiesta months, book 2 to 3 weeks early so your date is secured.

How do I reserve my date?

Call 0966 318 4942 or message us on Facebook with your event date, location, and the equipment you need. We’ll confirm availability and send your quote, and a small reservation fee may be needed to hold your date.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and common e-wallets such as GCash. Ask us about payment options when you book.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Just let us know as early as possible. We’ll do our best to move your booking to another available date.

Can I rent only one item?

Of course. You can rent a single item like a sound system or a few tables and chairs, or choose a package for the whole party.
